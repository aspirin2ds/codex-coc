## Useful rulings to verify

- Whether a roll should happen at all
- Whether a task is regular, hard, or extreme
- What failure means before allowing a push
- How much player narration to allow after success
- How to avoid dead ends when a clue check fails
- How to present madness effects without just removing agency for too long
