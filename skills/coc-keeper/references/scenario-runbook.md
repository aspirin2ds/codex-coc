# Scenario Runbook

Use this file after the investigators are ready.
It condenses the local Keeper draft for live play.

Primary source:
[docs/古茂密林之中-整理版.md](../../docs/古茂密林之中-整理版.md)

## Premise

The party joins a search after a ransom exchange collapses and kidnappers flee into the forest with Jane Strong.
The apparent crime story gradually reveals a deeper forest corruption tied to servants of 格拉基 and a crystal fragment.

## Truth the Keeper knows

- Kidnappers are only the outer layer of the threat.
- The forest has been under dream-guided influence for years.
- The real countdown is the transfer of the crystal to the lake on the third day.

## Recommended opening

Start in town on `1925-06-20`.
Frame the first playable scene as a briefing with:

- reward and urgency
- failed ransom exchange
- kidnappers fleeing into the woods
- rumors about hunters, artists, and recent strange activity

End the opening response with a practical choice such as:

- ask questions at the briefing
- buy supplies
- inspect the gunfight site
- talk to townsfolk before departure

## Three-act pacing

### Act 1: ordinary search

Player belief:

- this is a kidnapping pursuit
- the forest is dangerous but mundane

Keeper tasks:

- establish direction and reward
- give the party ways to prepare
- let success feel immediate and useful

### Act 2: wrongness spreads

Player belief shifts toward:

- something else is in the woods
- the kidnappers are no longer the whole story

Keeper tasks:

- escalate clues one notch at a time
- connect hunters, artists, trucks, and dreams
- keep moving even after failed rolls

### Act 3: deadline and rescue

Player belief becomes:

- Jane may still be alive
- the real threat is the lake and dig site
- waiting means losing

Keeper tasks:

- make sunset on day three feel like a hard timer
- support stealth, trickery, sabotage, rescue, or assault
- let the world advance if the players stall

## Scene ladder

Use these in rough order, but allow detours:

1. briefing in town
2. gunfight site and trailhead
3. hunter contact
4. first night dreams
5. artist body
6. truck and survey traces
7. second night pressure
8. kidnapper hideout
9. Turner cabin and strange paths
10. lake
11. dig site finale

## Fail-forward rules

- Failed `追踪` means lost time, fatigue, or danger, not a broken scenario.
- Failed social reads can cause suspicion or delay, but still leak usable clues.
- Failed stealth at the dig site can turn the finale into a prison-break or desperate rescue.

## Endings

- Good: Jane saved, prisoners freed, crystal transfer stopped.
- Mixed: Jane saved but the plan partly succeeds.
- Bad: the crystal reaches the lake and the horror escalates.

Apply sanity rewards or losses using the local scenario notes and confirm rule details through `$coc-rules-search` when needed.
